================================================================================
HUONG DAN CHIEN LUOC CHON: TOP PICK - VALUE BET - DARK HORSE
(QUANTITATIVE HORSE RACING SELECTION & BETTING GUIDE)
================================================================================

1. TOP PICK (UNG CU VIEN SO 1 - NGUA MANH NHAT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Dinh nghia:
  - Con ngua co Xac suat Thang (Win Probability) va Diem danh gia (Power Rating)
    cao nhat qua 10,000 luot chay mo phong Monte Carlo cua engine C++.

* KHI NAO NEN CHON:
  1. Xac suat thang vuot troi: Win Prob dat tu 35% - 50%+ (ti le ap dao).
  2. Co tu 2 Factor Badges tro len (vi du: "Peak Form", "Last Start Winner", 
     "Distance Specialist").
  3. Fair Odds (Gia niem yet hop ly) tu $2.00 - $3.50 va thi truong khong bi 
     ep gia qua sau (< $1.40).

* CHIEN THUAT DANH (BET TYPE):
  - Single Win: Cuoc Thang don.
  - Banker (Mo neo): Lam con chu luc de kep cho cac ve Place, Quinella (Top 2), 
    Exacta (Dung thu tu Top 2), Trifecta (Top 3).

* KHI NAO NEN TRANH:
  - Khi gia thi truong bi dam qua sau (vi du chi con $1.20 - $1.35) ma mat san 
    hoac thoi tiet bi thay doi dot ngot.


2. VALUE PICK / VALUE BET (KEO THOM - GIA TRI TOAN HOC CAO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Dinh nghia:
  - Con ngua co xac suat ve dich cao hon muc nha cai/thi truong danh gia.
  - Cong thuc Ky vong duong (+EV):
    EV = (Xac suat Thang cua C++ x Ty le cuoc thi truong) - 1 > 0

* KHI NAO NEN CHON:
  1. Mo hinh C++ tinh ra Fair Odds khoang $3.50 - $5.50 (Win Prob 20% - 30%), 
     nhung nha cai dang tra ty le cao hon dang ke (vi du: $6.50 - $9.00).
  2. Ngua co phong do on dinh (Rank 2 hoac Rank 3), thuong xuyen ve trong Top 3
     (Place Prob > 65%).
  3. Day la loai keo dem lai LOI NHUAN DAI HAN nhat theo nguyen ly xac suat.

* CHIEN THUAT DANH (BET TYPE):
  - Each-Way (EW): Dat cuoc ca Thang (Win) va Top 3 (Place). Neu ngua khong ve 
    Nhat nhung ve Nhi/Ba van co loi nhuan.
  - Flat Stake Win: Danh deu tay tren cac keo co Expected Value duong.


3. DARK HORSE (NGUA AN SO - KEO BAT NGO / TI LE AN CAO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Dinh nghia:
  - Con ngua co rank tong the bac trung (Rank 3 - 6), nhung so huu 1 DIEM MANH
    DAC THU CU THE co the tao nen dot bien trong ngay thi dau.

* KHI NAO NEN CHON:
  1. Dieu kien dac biet: Troi mua/san uot va ngua co huy hieu "Condition Specialist" 
     (Chuyen gia san uot), trong khi cac con top dau khong quen chay san lay.
  2. Loi the cong: O cu ly ngan (1000m-1200m) ngua co "Inside Draw Advantage" 
     (Xuat phat cong 1-2).
  3. Top Pick bi bat loi: Con Top 1 phai ganh trong luong qua nang (60kg+) hoac 
     boc tham trung cong ngoai cung (Barrier 10-14).
  4. Ty le cuoc thuong rat cao (Odds tu $9.00 - $25.00+).

* CHIEN THUAT DANH (BET TYPE):
  - Place Bet: Cuoc ve Top 3 voi ty le Place rat an tuong ($2.50 - $4.50).
  - Chan phu trong ve Exotic: Them vao chan 2, chan 3 cua ve Quinella / Trifecta
    de no hu tien thuong khong lo neu co bat ngo xay ra.

5. CHIEN THUOC KET HOP HOAN HAO (PORTFOLIO STRATEGY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trong 1 ngay dua:
- 60% Ngan sach : Danh vao cac keo TOP PICK co xac suat thang ap dao.
- 30% Ngan sach : Danh vao cac keo VALUE BET (Each-Way) de sinh loi dai han.
- 10% Ngan sach : Danh vao cac keo DARK HORSE (Place hoac ghep Trifecta) de 
                  san nhung phat dot bien ty le cao.
