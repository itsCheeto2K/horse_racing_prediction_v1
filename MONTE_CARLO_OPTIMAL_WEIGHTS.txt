================================================================================
HUONG DAN TI LE TRONG SO TOI UU TRONG MO PHONG MONTE CARLO (HORSE RACING)
================================================================================

1. TI LE CHUAN TOAN DIEN (MAC DINH - AP DUNG CHO DA SO CUOC DUA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Phong do gan day (Form Recency & Momentum)          : 30%
- Do thich nghi mat san (Track & Condition Affinity)  : 25%
- Cu ly & Trong luong ganh (Distance & Weight)         : 20%
- Hieu suat Nai ngua & HLV (Jockey & Trainer)         : 15%
- Loi the Cong xuat phat (Barrier Draw Bias)           : 10%
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tong cong                                             : 100%

* Ly do: 
  - Phong do va Mat san chiem 55% tong trong so vi day la 2 yeu to co tuong quan
    cao nhat toi ket qua thuc te. Ngua co phong do cao nhung gap mat san khong
    quen (vi du san lay Heavy) thuong co ti le truot top rat cao.


2. TINH CHINH THEO TUNG KICH BAN CU THE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. DUA NUOC RUT NGAN (Sprint 1000m - 1200m)
   - Dac diem: Toc do ban dau va vi tri xuat phat cuc ky quan trong.
   - Phong do (Form)             : 30%
   - Cong xuat phat (Barrier)    : 20%  [Tang len]
   - Cu ly (Distance)            : 20%
   - Mat san (Condition)         : 15%
   - Nai & HLV (Jockey/Trainer)  : 15%

B. SAN UOT / TROI MUA (Soft 6-7, Heavy 8-10)
   - Dac diem: Can uu tien ngua co kha nang "boi bun" (Wet Track Specialist).
   - Mat san (Condition)         : 35% - 40%  [Tang manh]
   - Phong do (Form)             : 25%
   - Cu ly (Distance)            : 15%
   - Nai & HLV (Jockey/Trainer)  : 15%
   - Cong xuat phat (Barrier)    : 5%   [Giam xuong]

C. DUA DUONG DAI / THE LUC (Staying > 1800m - 2400m)
   - Dac diem: Suc ben, trong luong ganh va chien thuat cua nai ngua quyet dinh.
   - Cu ly & Can nang (Distance) : 30%  [Tang len]
   - Phong do (Form)             : 25%
   - Mat san (Condition)         : 20%
   - Nai & HLV (Jockey/Trainer)  : 20%  [Tang len]
   - Cong xuat phat (Barrier)    : 5%   [Giam xuong]


3. SO LUOT MO PHONG MONTE CARLO (SIMULATIONS COUNT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 10,000 luot (Khuyen dung mac dinh):
  + Thoi gian tinh toan C++: ~0.01 giay.
  + Sai so phan phoi xac suat: < 0.3%.
  + Toi uu cho hau het moi cuoc dua.

- 25,000 luot (Do chinh xac cao):
  + Ap dung khi cuoc dua co tren 14 ngua hoac can xac dinh ti le Value Bet
    (keo thom / ngua tiem nang) voi do tin cay toan hoc toi da.
